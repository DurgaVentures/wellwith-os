Dashboard figures are working-paper indicators from records currently loaded in WELLWITH OS. Potential ITC is not confirmed credit, and net GST payable is not calculated here. Verify place of supply, GSTR-2B, statutory eligibility and reverse-charge tax before filing.

GST filing workflow

  1. Verify sales invoices, GSTINs, place of supply and HSN details.
  2. Record supplier bills with taxable value and tax breakup.
  3. Import GSTR-2B and investigate invoice and tax mismatches.
  4. Review eligible and ineligible ITC and reverse-charge transactions.
  5. Reconcile GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B working papers before portal filing.

WELLWITH OS prepares working papers only. It does not file returns or determine statutory ITC eligibility automatically.
